Where My Soul Belongs

Some things are simply meant to happen.

The soul is not trapped on the human body but is contained into something else. I believe that humans live their lives by doing the things that makes them happy and perhaps that object becomes the container of their soul. Just like how the guitarist feels as though they can not live without playing music with his guitar and the artist who can not stop painting he finds attractive.

And this leaves me pondering: What are the limitations of that container can be? How could the human soul fit within the thinest brush? and if the human soul can be contained into something we love, would it be able to survive into another living being? Just like how some of us choose animals as the medium through which we express our anima.

Perhaps the soul does not care about the form of that container. But seeks where it feels it belonged.

And somehow my soul found its way to you.

Don’t ask how, nor ask why.

Some things simply happen because they are destined to.
